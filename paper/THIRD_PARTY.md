# Unmodified IEEE formatting support

The following original files are included to keep the source portable:

- `IEEEtran.cls`, version 1.8b (2015-08-26), by Michael Shell and contributors.
  Downloaded from
  https://mirrors.ctan.org/macros/latex/contrib/IEEEtran/IEEEtran.cls .
- `IEEEtran.bst`, version 1.14 (2015-08-26), by Michael Shell and contributors.
  Downloaded from
  https://mirrors.ctan.org/macros/latex/contrib/IEEEtran/bibtex/IEEEtran.bst .

Both files are unmodified. Their original copyright, attribution, licensing,
and distribution notices remain in their headers. They are distributed under
the LaTeX Project Public License according to those notices. Including them
does not imply IEEE endorsement or acceptance of the manuscript.

Matplotlib-generated vector figures are original to this manuscript. Their
embedded DejaVu fonts are subsetted by Matplotlib. Application dependencies
and data artifacts are referenced in place, not relicensed here.
